The Sunday-night problem: the work got done all week. The invoices didn't. Somewhere between the truck, the group text, and the sticky note on the dash, billable work goes unbilled — and the rest gets invoiced days late, from memory.

The loop

Four steps. None of them are "type up invoices."

It's a sequence because your week is a sequence: schedule it, do it, bill it, get paid.

01 — SCHEDULE

Connect the calendar you already use

Google, Outlook, or Apple — paste one secret calendar address. ServiceBill syncs every 15 minutes and matches events to your QuickBooks customers. Anything it isn't sure about waits for a one-tap confirmation, then it remembers that match forever. Recurring visits work too: set a mow weekly or an inspection quarterly and a year of visits appears on the schedule.

02 — WORK

Your crew taps "done"

Each crew member sees their route on their phone. No app store, no accounts to manage — they sign in with your company number, their name, and a PIN. When a job's finished they tap complete, talk a quick note, snap photos, add extras.

03 — BILL

The invoice drafts itself

ServiceBill reads the event and the crew's note. "pine straw 15 bales" becomes 15 × your bale price, on the right customer, from your own QuickBooks catalog. You approve a queue, not a blank page.

04 — GET PAID

QuickBooks settles it

Approved invoices push to QuickBooks Online and email to the customer with a pay link — card, ACH, or Apple Pay, processed by QuickBooks Payments. Payments reconcile in your books automatically. Your bookkeeper sees clean ledgers, not a shoebox.

Questions

The ones everyone asks.

Which calendars work?

Google Calendar, Outlook / Microsoft 365, and Apple iCloud — anything that publishes a standard calendar feed. You paste one secret address; we sync it every 15 minutes. Calendar providers refresh their published feeds on their own schedule, so brand-new events can take 15–60 minutes to appear. That lag is theirs, not ours.

Do I need QuickBooks?

Yes — QuickBooks Online, any plan. ServiceBill connects through Intuit's official API: we pull your customers and service catalog, push approved invoices, and payments reconcile automatically through QuickBooks Payments.

What do my crew members need?

A phone with a browser. They sign in with your company number, their first name, and a PIN you set. No app store downloads, no email accounts, nothing to install on day one of a new hire.

What happens when the AI gets something wrong?

Nothing goes to a customer without you. Calendar matches it isn't sure about wait in a review queue; drafted invoices wait for your approval; every quantity is editable. When you correct a match once, it remembers — recurring events stop asking entirely.

What does ServiceBill cost during beta?

Nothing. Founding customers use the full product free while we finish the rough edges. When paid plans start you'll get clear notice and founding-customer pricing — we will never start charging your card by surprise, because we don't have your card.
